Exploring Reiki, IET and Advanced Energy Healing Therapies

Energy Healing includes a broad range of gentle practices intended to support relaxation, energetic balance, emotional awareness and spiritual connection.

Asttarte draws from decades of training and experience with Reiki, Integrated Energy Therapy, Igili Energy Therapy, acupressure, Chakra Healing and intuitive energy-balancing practices. Sessions are personalized according to each client’s needs, comfort, intentions and previous experience.

Energy Healing is not about forcing an emotional release or promising to cure a physical or psychological condition. It provides a supportive environment in which you can slow down, become more aware of what you are carrying and reconnect with your own inner resources.

Integrated Energy Therapy, commonly known as IET, combines gentle touch with focused attention to specific areas of the body and energy field.

Within an IET session, the practitioner may work with acupressure-related points and hand movements intended to support the release of emotional and energetic patterns. Clients often seek IET when they feel emotionally burdened, energetically depleted or interested in exploring the connection between their present experiences and unresolved patterns from the past.

IET is traditionally associated with angelic energy and heart-centered spiritual healing. Asttarte approaches this work with compassion, intuitive awareness and respect for each client’s personal spiritual beliefs.

Igili Energy Therapy is a gentle, light-touch energy-healing practice developed and taught by Victoria Pendragon. Its spiritual teachings describe a connection with a group of angelic beings called the Igili.

Unlike approaches that focus primarily on removing or releasing energy, Igili work is intended to support awareness, integration and transformation. The practitioner offers compassionate energetic support while the client notices sensations, feelings or insights that may emerge.
