Chakra Healing Sessions: What to Expect From Private Energy Healing

Have you explored Chakra Healing through meditation, Reiki, yoga or other energy practices and wondered what it might be like to experience this work in a private session?

Chakra Healing has been part of my energy-healing practice since I first began this work in the 1990s. I became a Reiki Master in 1997, during a time when Reiki and chakra work were far less familiar within mainstream wellness settings than they are today.

Over the years, chakra awareness has remained one of the practices I return to when helping people explore the relationship between their bodies, emotions, energy and spiritual lives.

In yogic and energy-healing traditions, the chakras are understood as subtle energy centers extending from the base of the spine through the crown of the head. Each chakra is traditionally associated with different qualities and aspects of our human experience.

Rather than thinking of Chakra Healing as something that must produce a particular sensation or immediate result, I approach it as a practice of awareness. Where do you feel grounded or unsettled? Open or guarded? Energized or depleted? Where might your body, emotions or energy be asking for greater attention?

Chakra work can become a continuing practice. As you become more familiar with your own energy, you may begin noticing subtle shifts in how you feel, respond and relate to yourself.

A private Chakra Healing session is individualized rather than performed according to one fixed formula.

You do not need previous experience with chakras, Reiki or meditation. You also don't need to arrive already knowing which chakra needs attention. We can begin with what you are actually experiencing and allow the session to unfold from there.

One of the things I love about Chakra Healing is how naturally it can complement other holistic and spiritual practices.

Chakra awareness can be incorporated into practices such as Reiki, meditation, yoga, Breathwork, Sound Healing, traditional and spiritual Tantra practices, energy work and crystal healing. As a Certified Tantra Teacher and Reiki Master, I may incorporate aspects of meditation, breath, embodiment and energy awareness from these traditions when appropriate to the individual and the intention of the session.

Sound can be incorporated through toning, singing bowls or other vibrational practices. Breathwork and meditation can help bring awareness inward. Crystals, visualization, movement and other practices can provide additional ways of exploring your relationship with your energy.

The session doesn't have to be about doing everything at once. The practices we use can be selected according to what feels appropriate for you.

Chakra work does not have to end when a private session ends.

You can explore the chakras through meditation, visualization, Breathwork, sound, movement and other practices at home. Developing a regular practice can give you opportunities to become more familiar with your own internal experience over time.

You may choose to focus on one chakra at a time or move through all seven primary chakras during a meditation.

The intention isn't to force yourself into a perfectly "balanced" state. Instead, the practice can become another way of listening—to your body, your emotions, your energy and yourself.
