Belly Massage for Relaxation, Emotional Release & Body Awareness

The belly can be one of the most vulnerable areas of the body to receive touch.

Many of us unconsciously tighten or protect the abdomen when we are stressed, uncomfortable, overwhelmed or emotionally guarded. We may hold our breath, contract the abdominal muscles or simply feel disconnected from this part of ourselves.

Gentle belly massage can offer an opportunity to slow down, breathe, become more aware of the body and experience nurturing professional touch within clear boundaries.

In my private holistic healing sessions, belly massage may be incorporated as part of bodywork, Somatic Stress Release, Breathwork, Reiki or other complementary practices when it is appropriate for the individual and something they are comfortable receiving.

The intention is not to force the body to release.

It is to create enough space to listen to what the body is experiencing.

Some people immediately notice tight shoulders or a tense jaw. Others may recognize stress through changes in breathing, digestive sensations, abdominal tension or a feeling of bracing through the center of the body.

Sometimes we don't notice how much we're holding until we slow down.

During a private session, bringing gentle awareness to the abdomen can help you begin noticing:

There isn't a particular response that you're supposed to have.

Body awareness begins with noticing what is actually present.

When we feel stressed or guarded, breathing may become shallow or restricted. We may hold the abdomen tightly without consciously realizing we're doing it.

Before massage even begins, I may invite you simply to notice your breathing.

Can you allow the abdomen to soften without forcing it?

Gentle massage combined with conscious breathing can create an opportunity to experience this part of the body differently.

Bodywork can occasionally bring emotions closer to our awareness.

A person may notice sadness, frustration, grief, vulnerability, relief or another feeling while receiving massage.

Someone else may experience nothing emotional at all and simply enjoy feeling relaxed.
