Reiki & Energy Clearing in Philadelphia | Asttarte Deva

Life can bring periods of stress, grief, emotional overwhelm, relationship changes and disconnection from ourselves. During these times, Reiki and Energy Healing can provide a quiet, supportive space to slow down, reconnect with your body and restore a greater sense of balance.

Asttarte offers personalized Reiki and Energy Clearing sessions for people who would like to release accumulated stress, deepen their spiritual practice, balance their energy and reconnect with their inner clarity and strength.

Reiki is a gentle energy-healing practice that encourages relaxation, energetic balance and mind-body awareness. During a session, Asttarte works intuitively with Reiki and other complementary energy-healing practices based on your needs, comfort and intentions.

All Reiki sessions are professional and nonsexual. Clients remain fully clothed, and sessions may include gentle touch or hands-off energy work according to your comfort and preferences.

Moving through grief, heartbreak or a major life transition

Feeling energetically depleted, ungrounded or disconnected

Seeking greater relaxation, clarity and emotional balance

Interested in exploring your chakras, energy field or intuitive awareness

You do not need previous experience with Reiki, Meditation or Energy Healing. Sessions are personalized for both beginners and people with an established spiritual practice.

In-person Reiki and Energy Healing sessions are available with Asttarte in Manayunk, Philadelphia.

Distance sessions are also available for people who prefer to receive support from home or who live outside the Philadelphia area. Online sessions may include distance Reiki, Chakra Healing, Guided Meditation, Breathwork, coaching and time for reflection and integration.

A single Reiki session can offer meaningful relaxation and insight. However, ongoing sessions provide more time to build trust, explore recurring emotional and energetic patterns, develop supportive practices and integrate what emerges between appointments.

3 sessions — a foundational introduction and minimum starting series

5 sessions — the recommended starting pathway for ongoing support

10 sessions — for deeper emotional, energetic and spiritual work

20 sessions — for longer-term healing, integration and personal development

Your pathway may focus primarily on Reiki or thoughtfully combine Reiki with Chakra Healing, Somatic Stress Release, Breathwork, Meditation, Sound Healing or Transformational Life Coaching.

Every person’s needs and intentions are different. The first step is a conversation with Asttarte to discuss what you are experiencing, what kind of support you are seeking and which session pathway may be most appropriate for you.
