Healy Frequency Wellness: Vibrational Support for Energy, Relaxation & Balance

Frequency-based wellness has been part of my exploration of energy healing for many years. Alongside practices such as Reiki, Sound Healing, Meditation, Breathwork and other forms of vibrational healing, I have also incorporated technology into my work.

One of the tools I continue to use is the Healy frequency device.

Healy offers a variety of frequency programs that can be selected according to the intention of the session. I sometimes incorporate the device into private, in-person sessions at Asttarte Deva Healing Wellness Center as another complementary tool within a larger holistic wellness experience.

Earlier versions of the system allowed me to use scanning and remote features differently than I am currently able to use them. At this time, I am not currently offering Healy scans or remote Healy sessions.

I can, however, continue using the device in person with clients, selecting from its available programs according to the intention of the session and the client's wellness goals.

Depending upon the person and the session, Healy may be incorporated alongside other complementary practices rather than being the entire focus of our time together.

My interest in Healy grew naturally from decades of working with energy and vibrational practices.

Different traditions approach the concept of energy in different ways. Reiki practitioners may speak about energy flow. Chinese Medicine traditions use concepts such as qi. Yogic traditions may speak about prana and chakras. Sound Healing works with vibration through sound and resonance.

Healy approaches wellness through frequency-based technology.

I don't view these approaches as identical. Rather, they are different ways of exploring the relationship between body awareness, relaxation, energy, vibration and well-being.

Rather than using an automated scan to tell us what someone needs, we begin with something much more human:

Are you interested in energy awareness or vibrational wellness?

From there, I can select an appropriate available Healy program and determine whether incorporating the device makes sense within the session.

The emphasis is on your stated intentions rather than presenting the technology as diagnosing what is happening inside your body.
