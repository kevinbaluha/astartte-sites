Becoming the Master of Your Own Energy: Reiki, Somatic Healing & Embodiment

Relieving stress, balancing your energy and learning practices that help you reconnect with yourself.

Healing is not about becoming dependent on another person as the source of your relief, connection or well-being. My intention is to help you develop a deeper relationship with your own body, breath, emotions, energy and inner wisdom.

Through Reiki, Advanced Energy Healing, Somatic Stress Release, Breathwork, Meditation and other holistic practices, you can begin recognizing what supports you, what overwhelms you and how to return to a more grounded and connected relationship with yourself.

This is what it means to become the master of your own energy: not controlling every feeling or experience, but developing the awareness and tools to meet yourself with greater presence, compassion and choice.

The first one to three sessions establish the foundation for our work together.

This applies whether you are completely new to my practice or returning after a significant period of time. Your present needs may be different from what they were when we previously worked together, so we begin by discussing what you are experiencing now.

Determining which combination of modalities may support you

There is no single sequence that is appropriate for everyone. We choose a starting point based on your needs, comfort, experience and goals.

If you are experiencing considerable stress, anxiety, grief or emotional overwhelm, we may begin with gentle Meditation, grounding, Breathwork or Somatic Stress Release practices.

If you are seeking relaxation, spiritual reconnection or energetic balance, we may begin with Reiki, Chakra Healing or another form of Energy Healing.

These modalities are not presented as a rigid ladder. Your healing pathway can evolve as we learn what genuinely supports you.

Reiki is a gentle energy-healing practice that can encourage relaxation, grounding and greater awareness of the relationship between your physical body, emotions and energy.

During a Reiki session, you remain fully clothed. Sessions may include light touch or hands-off energy work according to your preferences and consent.

Rather than promising to remove every difficult feeling or cleanse every cell of your body, Reiki offers a supportive environment in which you may slow down, notice what you are carrying and reconnect with yourself.

Every session is individualized. You are encouraged to communicate about your comfort, sensations and emotional experience throughout the process.

Stress and emotion can be experienced throughout the body. A person may notice tightness in the chest, tension in the shoulders, sensations in the abdomen or a feeling of being disconnected from certain parts of themselves.
