Guided Meditation for Pain Management & Relaxation

Meditation can be a supportive complementary practice for people living with physical discomfort, tension or stress. This guided meditation is an older recording from my teaching library, but the foundational practice remains relevant today.

The meditation invites you to slow down, connect with your breath and body, and develop greater awareness around sensations of discomfort without forcing the body to change.

Over the years, meditation has remained an important part of my holistic work and may also be incorporated into private sessions alongside Somatic Stress Release, Breathwork, Reiki & Energy Healing, Sound Healing, Yoga and other supportive practices.

Watch the guided Pain Management Meditation below.

For individualized Meditation and holistic wellness support, private sessions are available through Asttarte Deva Healing Wellness Center in Manayunk and online.

Meditation is a complementary wellness practice and is not intended to diagnose or treat medical conditions or replace appropriate medical care. Persistent or severe pain should be evaluated by an appropriate healthcare professional.
