Meet Asttarte

Holistic healing practitioner, teacher, and spiritual guide — more than three decades of practice.

Asttarte Deva is a holistic healing practitioner, teacher, and spiritual guide with more than three decades of experience in healing, meditation, and personal transformation.

Her work brings together Reiki and Advanced Energy Healing, advanced massage, Somatic Stress Release Therapy, breathwork, meditation, yoga, transformational life coaching, and intuitive spiritual practices. She offers a compassionate, individualized approach that honors the emotional, energetic, physical, and spiritual dimensions of each person.

The white tiger is a personal symbol for Asttarte. Born in the Year of the Tiger, she connects with white tiger energy as an expression of strength, intuition, courage, protection, and transformation.

Asttarte offers private sessions in Manayunk, Philadelphia, along with distance and online sessions, classes, and community events.
