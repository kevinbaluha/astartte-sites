# YouTube Content Strategy for Love as Medicine (Asttarte Deva)

**Channel:** Love as Medicine (youtube.com/user/AsttarteDeva or @asttarte)  
**Current subscribers:** ~340 (as of late 2026)  
**Primary Goal:** Generate qualified traffic to https://www.asttartedeva.com (wellness center services) and Calendly bookings while building E-E-A-T and authority.  
**Brand Alignment:** 
- Channel = "Love as Medicine" for deeper personal stories, teachings, transparency, and reflections.
- Drive to **Asttarte Deva Healing Wellness Center** site for services (professional, trauma-informed, boundaries-focused).
- Light cross-promotion to Substack (loveasmedicine.substack.com) for writings.
- **Never** promote outdated framing; use current service language.

**Core Principles (2026 Best Practices)**
- Shorts = Discovery engine (new viewers, algorithm reach).
- Long-form = Trust & conversion engine (watch time, authority, leads).
- "Barbell" approach: 1 long-form/week + 3–5 Shorts/week (repurpose from the same filming session).
- Hook in first 1–3 seconds.
- Strong, specific CTAs to website pages + Calendly.
- Captions/subtitles on everything (many watch muted).
- E-E-A-T: Lean into 25–30+ years experience, personal stories, clear boundaries, what-to-expect.
- Disclaimers: Always include "This is not medical or psychotherapy advice. Sessions are professional and trauma-informed. Consult your healthcare providers."

**Recommended Cadence (Sustainable for One Person)**
- **Weekly:** 1 long-form video (8–20 minutes).
- **Weekly:** 4–6 YouTube Shorts (15–60 seconds, vertical).
- Batch film: Shoot 1–2 long-form + cut Shorts from the same footage.
- Post Shorts on different days for consistent presence.
- Playlists: Group by pillar (e.g., "Breathwork for Healing", "Intimacy & Relationships").
- Series: "What to Expect in a Session", "One Thing I Tell Every Client", "My Healing Journey – Transparency Series".

**Content Pillars** (Tied to current site services + channel topics)
1. Intimacy, Attachment & Relationship Coaching (trauma-informed, couples/women/men)
2. Breathwork & Somatic Nervous System Work (including integration)
3. Energy Healing (Reiki Traditions, Other Forms of Energy Healing, IEET)
4. Somatic & Holistic Bodywork / Massage

8. Cacao Ceremonies & Heart-Opening
9. Personal Transparency / Healing Journey / E-E-A-T (builds deep trust)
10. Boundaries, Consent & What to Expect (strong trust signals)

**Traffic Generation Tactics (Every Video)**
- Description: First 2–3 lines hook + key links. Full chapters/timestamps. Links to specific service page (e.g., https://www.asttartedeva.com/services/intimacy-coaching/), Calendly, Substack (light), Instagram.
- End screens & cards: Link to long-form or specific playlist/page.
- Pinned comment: Direct link + "Book a session here: [Calendly]".
- Thumbnails: Your face + bold text (question or benefit) + calm/professional aesthetic. Consistent branding.
- Titles: Question or curiosity-driven + keywords (e.g., "What Breathwork Actually Does for Grief and Emotional Release").
- Hashtags in description: #Breathwork #IntimacyCoaching #EnergyHealing #SomaticHealing (mix broad + specific).
- Cross-promote: Mention in Instagram Stories/Reels, Linktree, site footer if relevant.
- Measure: Track traffic from YouTube in site analytics to specific pages/Calendly.

## YouTube Shorts Strategy (Discovery + Funnel)

**Format:** Vertical (9:16), 15–60 seconds. Hook immediately. Captions on screen. Fast pace. End with clear CTA.

**Script Template (Shorts)**
```
[0-3s HOOK - Big text on screen + spoken]
"Most people think [common myth or pain point]..."

[3-15s VALUE - Quick insight or tip from your experience]
"One thing I tell every client is..."

[15-45s TEASE or DEMO - Brief story, technique snippet, or explanation]
"Here's why it works..." or short guided breath or boundary example.

[End - Strong CTA]
"Full session breakdown on my latest long video [or link in description]. Book a private session at [Calendly link in bio/description]."

[On-screen text + spoken]
"Link in description 👇"
```

**10 Example Shorts Ideas (Script Outlines – Adapt from Your Copy/Experience)**
1. **Intimacy Hook:** "Why 'just communicate more' doesn't fix attachment wounds." → Quick distinction between surface talk and nervous system safety. CTA to intimacy page.
2. **Breathwork:** "One breath practice I use when grief feels stuck in the body." (30s demo + disclaimer). CTA to breathwork page.
3. **Boundaries:** "What I always say before a somatic or bodywork session." (Consent language). CTA to massage or somatic page.
4. **Energy Healing:** "The difference between Reiki and the other energy practices I offer." Quick contrast. CTA to reiki/energy page.
5. **Sound Healing:** "Why crystal bowls + drums together create deeper release." Short sound reference. CTA to sound page.
6. **Tarot/Readings:** "One question I get asked most in intuitive readings." Quick insight. CTA to tarot page.
7. **Personal Story (E-E-A-T):** "How losing my husband taught me about true intimacy work." 45s vulnerable clip. CTA to about or intimacy.
8. **Yoga/Meditation:** "The pose I recommend most for beginners who feel unsafe in their body." CTA to private yoga.
9. **Cacao:** "What cacao actually does in a ceremony (beyond the chocolate)." CTA to cacao page.
10. **Myth Bust:** "Is somatic work the same as tantric massage? Here's the truth." (Strong boundaries language). CTA to relevant service.

**Production Tips for Shorts**
- Film in one take or easy cuts.
- Use site FAQs and your PDF copy for accurate language.
- Repurpose 1 long-form into 4–6 Shorts.
- Post 3–5x/week for algorithm momentum.

## Long-Form Video Strategy (Trust + Conversions)

**Length:** 8–20 minutes. Good retention = algorithm loves it.
**Structure Template**
1. **Hook (0–15s):** Question, bold statement, or personal story hook.
2. **Intro + Authority (15–60s):** Who you are + years of experience + what we'll cover.
3. **Main Content:** Break into clear sections (use chapters).
4. **Real Examples/Stories:** Anonymized client patterns (with permission tone).
5. **Boundaries & What to Expect:** Critical for trust.
6. **Common Questions / Myths.**
7. **CTA:** Specific – "If this resonates, book a session here [Calendly]. Full details on the [service] page."
8. **Outro:** Thank you + subscribe for more.

**Script Outline Examples (Ready to Expand with Your Voice/Copy)**

**Example 1: Service Explainer – "What Is Trauma-Informed Intimacy & Relationship Coaching?" (12–15 min)**
- Hook: "If you've ever felt like you keep repeating the same relationship patterns no matter how much you 'work on it'..."
- Your background (3+ decades).
- What it is vs. what it's not.
- Who it's for (couples, women, men).
- What happens in a session.
- Boundaries statement (word-for-word from site).
- How it differs from therapy or coaching you've tried.
- CTA: Link to /services/intimacy-coaching/ + Calendly.
- Chapters in description.

**Example 2: "What Actually Happens in a Breathwork Session" (10–12 min)**
- Hook: "People come to breathwork expecting relaxation and leave surprised by what the body releases."
- Nervous system basics (simple, not overwhelming).
- Seated vs. other styles.
- Safety, preparation, integration.
- Personal story or client pattern example.
- Disclaimers.
- CTA to breathwork page + "Book here."

**Example 3: Personal Transparency – "My Healing Journey and Why I Do This Work" (15+ min)**
- Builds E-E-A-T.
- Tie to current services (how personal experience informs professional boundaries).
- End with invitation to work together.

**Other Long-Form Ideas**
- "The 5 Principles of Reiki Traditions I Still Teach Today"
- "How Sound Healing Supports Emotional Release (and What to Expect)"
- "Private Yoga Sessions: Why One-on-One Changes Everything"
- Q&A style: Answer 5 real client questions from your emails/FAQs.
- "Why I No Longer Call It [Old Term] and What I Offer Instead" (if relevant for transparency).

**Production Workflow**
1. Choose topic from pillar + current client questions or site FAQs.
2. Script outline (use templates above).
3. Film (talking head + calm background or simple visuals).
4. Edit: Chapters, captions, end screen.
5. Thumbnail + title.
6. Description with links, timestamps, disclaimers.
7. Cut Shorts same day or next.
8. Promote lightly on other platforms.
