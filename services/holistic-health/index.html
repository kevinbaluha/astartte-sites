Holistic Health

Personalized support for emotional, physical, energetic, and spiritual well-being after decades of work in the healing arts.

After decades in the holistic healing arts, Asttarte offers additional support for people seeking greater balance in everyday life—emotionally, physically, energetically, and spiritually.

Holistic Health sessions are personalized. They may integrate conversation, lifestyle reflection, nervous-system awareness, energy practices, and referrals to complementary care when appropriate. This work is supportive and educational; it is not a substitute for licensed medical or mental-health treatment.

If you feel called to explore this kind of support, begin with a consultation so the session can be shaped around what you need now.
