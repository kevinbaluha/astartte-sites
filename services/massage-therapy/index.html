A Whole-Person Approach to Massage

Stress does not affect only one part of us. It can influence the muscles, breath, emotions, energy, posture and the way we experience our bodies.

Holistic Massage creates time to slow down and notice what your body may be communicating. Touch, pressure, gentle movement, breathing and stillness may be combined to encourage relaxation and greater awareness. Your session may focus on:

  • General relaxation and stress reduction
  • Muscle tension and physical discomfort
  • Grounding and nervous-system support
  • Reconnecting with your body
  • Developing greater awareness of breath and sensation
  • Emotional well-being during periods of change
  • Restoring a sense of balance and inner spaciousness

This work is collaborative. You are welcome to communicate what feels supportive, request an adjustment or withdraw consent at any time.

Approaches That May Be Incorporated

Somatic Massage

Somatic Massage combines Asttarte’s Somatic Stress Release Therapy technique with an on-the-body healing process, similar to the deep inner emotional and spiritual work experienced during seated Somatic Stress Release sessions.

This approach may help release energy and emotions the body is carrying while receiving the nurturing touch of massage to support and integrate the somatic healing process. This is an active, collaborative experience where you fully participate alongside the practitioner. Rather than simply lying down, relaxing, and receiving massage, we are doing the healing work together.

Somatic Massage combines therapeutic touch with awareness of breath, feelings, physical sensations, and the body’s responses. The purpose is not simply to work on muscles, but to help you become more present, connected, and aware of your body and the emotions you may be holding. Sessions may include lighter or firmer pressure depending upon your individual needs, comfort, and preferences.

Belly Massage for Intimacy & Emotional Healing

Releasing Emotional Layers • Reconnecting With Your Body • Opening to Love

The belly is one of the most sensitive and vulnerable areas of the body. It is often where we experience our deepest emotions, from fear, anxiety, anger, and grief to love, passion, trust, and connection.

Throughout our lives, we may develop protective patterns that influence how we experience our bodies, express our emotions, and allow ourselves to receive love and intimacy.

Belly Massage is a gentle, intentional, body-centered healing practice that combines therapeutic touch, somatic awareness, breathwork, and emotional exploration to help you reconnect with the deeper feelings and sensations within your body.

Unlike traditional abdominal massage, which may focus primarily on muscles and physical tension, this approach invites you to actively participate in the experience through conscious breathing, awareness of sensations, emotional reflection, and compassionate self-inquiry.

Exploring the Emotional Layers Beneath the Surface

Sometimes the feelings we struggle to express in everyday life become intertwined with the way we experience tension, vulnerability, and protection in our bodies. Through gentle belly massage and guided somatic awareness, we may explore themes such as:

  • Fear and mistrust: Recognizing protective patterns that may make it difficult to relax, feel safe, or open to others.
  • Hidden anger and resentment: Exploring emotions that have been difficult to acknowledge, express, or resolve.
  • Grief and emotional pain: Creating space to compassionately acknowledge loss, disappointment, and heartache.
  • Intimacy and attachment wounds: Understanding fears of rejection, abandonment, vulnerability, and emotional closeness.
  • Self-love and receiving love: Exploring beliefs and emotional barriers that may interfere with feeling worthy of care, affection, and connection.
  • Passion and emotional longing: Discovering the deeper needs beneath familiar patterns of seeking love, validation, or connection.
  • Inner child healing: Gently exploring earlier emotional experiences that may continue to influence our relationships and sense of safety.

The intention is not to force an emotional release, but to create a supportive environment where feelings can be recognized, understood, and integrated at your own pace.

Shamanic Healing, Energy Awareness & Kundalini

My approach to Belly Massage has been deeply influenced by my experiences with shamanic healing, traditional plant medicine ceremonies, and spiritual practices that explore the relationship between the body, emotions, energy, and consciousness. Through these experiences, I came to appreciate how intentional touch, breath, and compassionate presence can support profound personal reflection and a deeper relationship with the body.

Within certain spiritual traditions, the abdomen is understood as an important center of life-force energy, personal power, and emotional awareness. In this work, we may explore energetic patterns, kundalini awareness, and what some spiritual traditions describe as karmic or deeply rooted patterns that influence our relationships and emotional experiences.

These concepts are approached as spiritual frameworks for personal exploration rather than medical explanations of physical or psychological conditions.

Reconnecting With Love, Trust & Intimacy

One of the deeper intentions of this work is to explore the protective layers that may stand between us and our capacity to experience love.

Sometimes we long for closeness while simultaneously fearing it. We may desire affection but struggle to receive it, or seek connection from others while feeling disconnected from ourselves.

Through intentional touch, guided breathwork, and emotional awareness, Belly Massage offers an opportunity to explore these experiences with compassion. This work may support you in developing a more trusting relationship with your body, strengthening self-acceptance, and becoming more aware of the emotional patterns influencing how you give and receive love.

Combining Belly Massage With Cacao Healing

Belly Massage can be incorporated into a personalized heart-centered session that includes a guided cacao ceremony.

Ceremonial cacao, meditation, intention-setting, breathwork, and somatic exploration can complement one another in creating a reflective experience focused on self-compassion, emotional awareness, and personal growth.

Together, these practices invite you to explore the connection between your heart, belly, breath, emotions, and inner experience.

What Happens During a Session

Before beginning, we discuss:

  • What you are seeking from the session
  • Areas of tension or sensitivity
  • Pressure preferences
  • Areas you would like included or avoided
  • Preferences concerning touch, energy work and other supportive practices
  • Any physical conditions or concerns that may affect the session

You remain appropriately draped throughout table-based massage. Only the area being worked on is uncovered, and intimate areas are never included. Depending upon your needs, the session may incorporate massage, compression, acupressure, supported stretching, Breathwork, grounding or hands-on or hands-near energy healing.

There is no requirement to experience an emotional release. Quiet relaxation, rest and increased body awareness are meaningful outcomes in themselves.

Consent and Professional Boundaries

All massage and bodywork offered by Asttarte Deva Healing Wellness Center is professional, therapeutic, and supportive of each client’s individual healing journey. Sessions do not include erotic massage, sexualized touch by the practitioner, or requests concerning the practitioner’s clothing, appearance, or personal participation in intimate activities. All practices are guided by professional boundaries, informed consent, mutual respect, and each client’s individual needs and readiness.

Consent is ongoing. You may request lighter pressure, different positioning, hands-off energy work or the end of a technique or session at any time. New clients complete a phone consultation before scheduling so that we can discuss their needs, answer questions and determine whether the work is an appropriate fit.
