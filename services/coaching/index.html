Services

Coaching

Transformational life coaching, relationship coaching, emotional intimacy coaching, couples support, and grief-informed emotional healing.

Asttarte works with individuals and couples seeking meaningful change in their emotional lives, relationships, spiritual development, and connection with themselves. Sessions are personalized and may incorporate conversation, somatic awareness, meditation, breath, and energy practices when supportive.

Areas of focus

  • Transformational life coaching — goals, transitions, confidence, self-trust, and patterns that keep repeating.
  • Relationship coaching — communication, attachment patterns, conflict, and healthier ways of relating.
  • Emotional intimacy coaching — closeness, vulnerability, desire, and emotional availability.
  • Couples coaching — shared understanding, repair, and intentional partnership work.
  • Grief and emotional healing — support through loss, heartbreak, and major life change.

Coaching may be offered independently or combined with meditation, breathwork, somatic practices, or energy healing.
