Reiki & Energy Healing

Practices similar to Reiki for relaxation, emotional well-being, and spiritual connection.

Personalized Support for Relaxation, Emotional Well-Being and Spiritual Connection

Reiki and energy healing offer gentle ways to slow down, reconnect with yourself and bring compassionate attention to areas that feel stressed, depleted or out of balance.

Each session is personalized and may incorporate Reiki, chakra balancing, Integrated Energy Therapy, intuitive energy healing, meditation, sound or other supportive practices. Some people experience warmth, relaxation, emotion, imagery or subtle sensations, while others simply feel rested and peaceful.

Reiki lineages, including Lotus Light, are described on the Reiki Traditions page. This page is for practices similar to Reiki.

Chakra Awareness & Balancing

Chakra balancing focuses on the seven primary energy centers described within chakra traditions. A session may include hands-on or hands-near energy work, intuitive awareness, meditation, visualization or a pendulum. The intention is to support greater balance, grounding and connection with yourself.

Chakra Energy Healing

A focused chakra session works with one or more energy centers connected with the concerns you bring to the session. The practice may support greater awareness of your emotions, energy and spiritual life while encouraging a subjective sense of balance and alignment.
