Personalized Support for Relaxation, Emotional Well-Being and Spiritual Connection

Reiki and energy healing offer gentle ways to slow down, reconnect with yourself and bring compassionate attention to areas that feel stressed, depleted or out of balance.

Each session is personalized and may incorporate Reiki, chakra balancing, Integrated Energy Therapy, intuitive energy healing, meditation, sound or other supportive practices. Some people experience warmth, relaxation, emotion, imagery or subtle sensations, while others simply feel rested and peaceful.

Reiki lineages, including Lotus Light, are described on the Reiki Traditions page. This page is for practices similar to Reiki.

Chakra Awareness & Balancing

Chakra balancing focuses on the seven primary energy centers described within chakra traditions. A session may include hands-on or hands-near energy work, intuitive awareness, meditation, visualization or a pendulum. The intention is to support greater balance, grounding and connection with yourself.

Chakra Energy Healing

A focused chakra session works with one or more energy centers connected with the concerns you bring to the session. The practice may support greater awareness of your emotions, energy and spiritual life while encouraging a subjective sense of balance and alignment.

Cord Cutting Relationship Healing

Chakra connections with another person being released

Cord cutting is a guided spiritual practice for people who want to examine attachments, recurring relationship patterns or emotional connections that feel draining. Through meditation, visualization and energy work, the practice may support clearer boundaries and help you reclaim your attention and energy. It does not control another person or require ending a relationship.

Integrated Energy Therapy

Integrated Energy Therapy, or IET, is one of Asttarte’s favorite energy-healing modalities. She learned this practice from a dear friend and fellow Reiki practitioner who passed away approximately seven years ago, and she continues to honor their connection through this work.

During an IET session, Asttarte uses gentle, direct touch to hold specific acupressure and integration points associated with the body and particular emotional patterns. She draws heavy emotional energy out of the surrounding auric field and then brings supportive, positive energy back into each area.

The practice may work with distrust at the third eye and restore trust; guilt at the top of the head and restore innocence; shoulds and regrets at the shoulders and restore freedom; grief and a broken heart at the heart center and restore self-love; anger and resentment around the liver area and restore forgiveness; feelings of threat at the throat and restore safety; shame at the collarbone and restore spiritual pride; stress at the lower belly and restore power and ease; and fear at the hips and restore security.

IET may also support connecting with one’s angels and soul, releasing resistance, becoming open to receiving help and moving forward with greater freedom.

Asttarte's Signature Modality IEET℠ — Intuitive Emotional Energy Therapy

Intuitive Emotional Energy Therapy (IEET℠) is Asttarte’s signature guided-meditation and energy-healing modality, created in 2010 while she was participating in Landmark’s Introduction to Leadership Program. Its three interconnected techniques—Inner Child and Emotional Reconnection, Deep Relaxation Therapy and Quantum Intuitive Healing—support emotional release, reconnection with hidden or disconnected parts of yourself, and freedom from unhealthy attachments and energetic entanglements. Each seated session is intuitively guided according to what you are ready to explore.

Inner Child and Emotional Reconnection

Making peace with the inner child and hidden emotions

This seated guided process begins by locating an emotion or area of discomfort within the body.

Inner Child and Emotional Reconnection is a technique of the Intuitive Emotional Energy Therapy (IEET℠) the signature modality created by Asttarte (2010) to work with deep emotions and energetic patterns that may feel as though they linger within the body and auric field. Through intuitive awareness and personalized energy work, the session explores how these patterns may be affecting you emotionally, energetically and physically. The intention is to support emotional release, renewed energy flow and greater freedom to move forward.

Quantum Intuitive Healing

Releasing Attachments, Soul Entanglements & Unhealthy Connections

Quantum Intuitive Healing is a technique inside the IEET℠ modality, that works intuitively with areas of energy that feel blocked, heavy or dense. Asttarte focuses her awareness on the area while guiding you through a shared process of exploring, unraveling and clearing what is ready to shift. As greater space is created, you may experience increased receptivity, clarity, lightness or connection with yourself.
