Reiki & Energy Healing

Gentle Reiki and advanced energy work for relaxation, balance, emotional well-being, and spiritual connection. Modalities may include chakra healing, IET, IEET℠, cord cutting, and quantum intuitive healing.

Reiki and energy healing offer gentle ways to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and bring compassionate attention to areas that feel stressed, depleted, or out of balance.

Each session is personalized and may incorporate Reiki, chakra balancing, Integrated Energy Therapy (IET), Intuitive Emotional Energy Therapy (IEET℠), meditation, sound, or other supportive practices. Some people experience warmth, relaxation, emotion, imagery, or subtle sensations; others simply feel rested and peaceful.

Approaches that may be included

  • Chakra awareness & balancing — work with the primary energy centers through hands-on or hands-near practice, meditation, visualization, or pendulum.
  • Cord-cutting relationship healing — guided practice for examining draining attachments and reclaiming attention and energy, without controlling another person.
  • Integrated Energy Therapy (IET) — gentle direct touch at integration points, clearing heavy emotional energy and inviting supportive qualities such as trust, innocence, freedom, self-love, forgiveness, and safety.
  • IEET℠ — Intuitive Emotional Energy Therapy — Asttarte’s signature guided modality (created 2010), including Inner Child & Emotional Reconnection, Deep Relaxation Therapy, and Quantum Intuitive Healing.
  • Reiki healing — light touch or hands above the body, drawn from multiple traditions and adapted to you.

Sessions & pathways

Sessions are available in person in Manayunk, Philadelphia, and at a distance online. Single sessions typically range from $250 to $425 depending on length and format. Structured pathways of three, five, ten, and twenty sessions are available for continuing support.

All touch is optional and based on consent. Sessions can use light touch, hands above the body, or a combination you choose.

You do not need to know which modality to choose before contacting Asttarte. A phone consultation helps determine what may best support you.
