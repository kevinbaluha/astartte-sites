Yoga & Meditation

Private Yin Yoga, gentle yoga, and meditation for presence, embodiment, and deep relaxation—no flexibility required.

Yoga does not have to be fast, athletic, or physically demanding to be powerful. Private Yoga & Meditation sessions are designed for people who want a slower, more personalized way of connecting with their bodies.

You may be experiencing stiffness, tension, or discomfort. You may be stressed, tired, recovering, or simply craving quiet. The approach emphasizes Yin Yoga, gentle movement, conscious breathing, body awareness, meditation, and deep relaxation.

Rather than forcing your body into a particular posture or sequence, sessions work with the body you have today. Some sessions are almost entirely slow and restorative. Others may incorporate classical postures, gentle flowing movement, or elements of Vinyasa when appropriate.

You do not need to be flexible. You do not need previous yoga experience. You do not need to keep up with anyone else.

This is an opportunity to slow down, breathe, move with awareness, and develop a deeper relationship with your own body.
