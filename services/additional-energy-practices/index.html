Additional Energy Practices

Practices similar to Reiki that are not Reiki lineages.

Frequency Balancing

Frequency Balancing is an energy-healing technique that Asttarte may incorporate when she perceives heaviness or congestion within or around the body. She works with a focused field of energy—experienced visually as a bubble—over a specific area. As the energy begins to clear, she gradually makes the field smaller, bringing her hands closer to the body until the heaviness feels diminished or dissolved. She may also work along the body’s meridian lines to encourage energetic movement, clearing and balance.

Axiotonal Alignment

Axiotonal Alignment is an energy-healing practice that works with the body’s vertical and horizontal energetic pathways. Asttarte traces geometric lines and energetic points above and around the body, envisioning them as part of a greater universal grid extending beyond the individual. The practice is intended to clear and realign these pathways, strengthen energetic connection and support a greater sense of balance, openness and flow.

Igili Energy Healing

Igili Energy Healing is a hands-on practice in which Asttarte directs energy deeply into an area that feels stagnant, blocked or energetically dense. The energy is intended to penetrate the affected area, gradually breaking down and dissolving the heaviness so it can disperse naturally within the body’s energy field. Igili Energy may be incorporated into a personalized energy-healing session when deeper, focused support feels appropriate. Direct touch is always discussed beforehand and used only with the client’s consent.
