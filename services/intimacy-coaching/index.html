Intimacy Coaching

Private intimacy coaching for couples, women healing from heartache and attachment wounds, and men recovering connection, integrity, and love in the heart.

Intimacy coaching offers a compassionate, grounded space to understand what is happening in your heart, body, and relationships—and to rebuild closeness, honesty, and nervous-system safety at a pace that respects your story.

For couples

For partners who feel estranged, stuck in distance, or simply not as close as they long to be. Sessions support communication, emotional intimacy, repair after rupture, desire and connection, and creating shared practices that bring you back toward one another.

For women

For women navigating trauma and grief from heartache, lack of connection, attachment wounds, and nervous-system dysregulation—especially after a partner’s lack of integrity, cheating, or sex addiction. The work honors grief, boundary repair, body safety, and reclaiming your capacity for love without abandoning yourself.

For men

For men healing from sex addiction patterns, feeling lost, unable to find love in their hearts, grief, loneliness, and the longing to become a more present, honest partner to themselves and others. Sessions may weave coaching with somatic awareness, accountability, and heart-centered practices.

Deeper immersion offerings—including extended intimacy session formats—are detailed on Love as Medicine. Related writing lives on loveasmedicine.substack.com.
