Breathwork

Guided Breathwork Core Healing for emotional release, stress relief, embodiment, grief, inner-child work, and deeper connection with yourself.

Breathwork Core Healing is a personalized, guided practice that uses conscious breathing, meditation, body awareness, movement, sound, and emotional expression to help you reconnect with yourself and what you are experiencing.

People come to Breathwork carrying many different things—stress, emotional shutdown, disconnection from the body, grief, difficulty accessing feelings, or a sense that something inside wants movement.

A possible arc of the work

  1. Beginning — Activation — intention, grounding, and entering the breath.
  2. Intensity — Moving through stagnation — allowing held energy, emotion, or tension to move.
  3. Release — Nearing completion — expression, tears, sound, or quiet letting-go as it arises.
  4. Integration — Rest & peace — settling, reflection, and support for returning to daily life.

Some experiences are gentle and meditative. Others can become active, expressive, and physically or emotionally intense. You may experience tears, laughter, sound, movement, tingling, warmth, emotional awareness—or simply deep rest. All of it is welcome within consent and guidance.
