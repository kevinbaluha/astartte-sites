Tarot, Astrology & Intuitive Readings

Tarot • Astrology • Intuitive Guidance • Life Path • Relationships • Past Lives

Tarot, astrology, and intuitive readings can offer insight into the patterns, relationships, choices, and possibilities unfolding in your life. Through the symbolism of the Tarot, the wisdom of astrology, and intuitive guidance, Asttarte helps you explore what may be influencing your path and where greater clarity may be available.

Whether you are seeking insight around love and relationships, career, family, health and wellness, spiritual growth, past lives, or your life path, each reading is shaped around the questions and areas of your life that matter most to you.

Life Path Reading

Discover your soul's blueprint and the lessons you're here to learn. A Life Path Reading provides clarity about your purpose, your natural gifts, and the challenges that shape your journey. Gain direction and insight into the choices ahead so you can move forward with confidence and alignment.

Offered as a Tarot Reading, Intuitive Reading or Astrology Reading

Relationship Reading

Whether you're single, dating, or in a long-term partnership, a Relationship Reading sheds light on patterns of connection, communication, and intimacy. Career Reading

Find clarity on your professional path. A Career Reading helps you uncover opportunities for growth, align your work with your passions, and navigate challenges in the workplace. Whether you're considering a career change, looking for advancement, or seeking fulfillment in your current role, this reading offers grounded insight and direction.

A Health Reading provides a holistic perspective on the energetic and emotional factors influencing your well-being.
