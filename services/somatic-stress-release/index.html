Somatic Stress Release Therapy

Asttarte’s approach to Somatic Stress Release Therapy begins with safety, grounding and listening. Through seated conversation, gentle breathing, mindfulness, emotional exploration, imagery and intuitive energy awareness, each session follows what your body is ready to reveal. The images below illustrate some of the ways a session may unfold. Every experience is individual, and no particular sensation, emotion or release is expected.

Beginning with Grounding and Safety

We begin seated together in a comfortable space.

You are invited to share what you have been experiencing, which may include emotional distress, relationship or intimacy concerns, anxiety, physical discomfort or a general feeling that something within you is unsettled.

Asttarte then guides you through gentle breathing and grounding practices to help you become present in the room and more aware of your body. If your system feels highly activated, the initial focus may simply be helping you feel steadier, safer, more balanced and supported.

There is no pressure to immediately enter a deep emotional process.

Discovering What Feels Possible

As you become more settled, Asttarte will help you notice whether it feels comfortable to breathe more deeply and explore the sensations, emotions or energy within your body.

If inward exploration feels accessible, the session may move into a deeper Somatic Stress Release process. If it does not feel supportive that day, the session can remain conversational or shift into another modality, including table-based relaxation or energy work.

Your body’s response helps determine the direction and pace of the session.

Giving Sensations an Image and a Voice

A session may focus on a particular area of discomfort or tension. The neck and shoulders, for example, may feel as though they are carrying an enormous weight. The sensation might appear in your imagination as heavy bricks, a clamp, pressure, pins and needles or something entirely personal to you.

Rather than immediately trying to make the sensation disappear, Asttarte may invite you to notice it, describe it and breathe gently into it. As your body relaxes around the experience, feelings or words may begin to emerge. Intuitive questions and relational or emotional guidance may help you understand what the sensation has been carrying and what it may need from you.
