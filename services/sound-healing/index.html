Sound Healing

Individual and group sound healing with crystal and Tibetan bowls, drums, didgeridoo, voice, and deep listening.

Sound Healing sessions draw from instruments gathered and worked with over the years, including crystal singing bowls, Tibetan singing bowl, buffalo drum, ocean drum, didgeridoo, rain stick, tuning tools, and voice.

Each instrument creates a different quality of sound and vibration—soft and meditative, rhythmic, resonant, grounding, or expansive. Instruments are woven intuitively into the session according to what supports you.

You are invited to rest comfortably, close your eyes, and receive the sounds without needing to do anything. The practice becomes an opportunity to slow down, listen deeply, and allow periods of sound and silence to support relaxation, emotional release, energetic balance, and spiritual connection.

Rhythm, humming, toning, mantra, voice, or gentle instruments may also be incorporated into other private healing sessions when desired. No musical experience is required.
