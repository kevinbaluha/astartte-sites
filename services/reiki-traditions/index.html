Reiki Traditions

Asttarte is trained in several Reiki traditions, each with its own energetic qualities, symbolism and areas of focus. During a personalized session, she may draw from one tradition or intuitively combine several according to your intentions and what feels most supportive.

Reiki may be offered through light touch, with hands positioned above the body or through a combination chosen with your consent. You do not need to select a particular Reiki tradition before scheduling. Sessions may support relaxation, stress reduction, self-awareness and spiritual connection. Asttarte draws from her extensive Reiki training while adapting each session to the individual.

Usui Reiki

Healing from the inside out.

Usui Reiki is the foundational Reiki tradition and the basis for many systems that developed afterward. Its gentle, grounding quality may support relaxation, energetic balance, clarity and spiritual awareness.

Tera-Mei Seichem Reiki

Tera-Mei Seichem Reiki works with energetic qualities traditionally associated with earth, water, fire, air and spirit. It may be used to support grounding, emotional movement, energetic clearing and renewed vitality.

Shamballa Reiki

Shamballa Reiki has a strong, transformative and fire-like quality. It may be incorporated when someone wishes to bring compassionate awareness to deeply rooted emotions, old patterns or areas that feel guarded or stagnant.

Lotus Light Reiki

The Divine Mother

Lotus Light Reiki carries a soft, nurturing quality associated with Divine Mother energy, Mother Mary and Quan Yin. It may support self-love, tenderness, emotional comfort and compassionate inner-child healing.

Kundalini Reiki

clear the blocks to your flow

Kundalini Reiki works with the energy centers and the movement of energy through the body’s central channel. It may support energetic awareness, balance, vitality and spiritual connection.
