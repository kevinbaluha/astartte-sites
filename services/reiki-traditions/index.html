Reiki Traditions

Usui, Tera Mai Seichem, Shamballa, Lotus Light, Kundalini Reiki, and complementary energy-healing tools—combined intuitively for your session.

Asttarte is trained in several Reiki traditions, each with its own energetic qualities, symbolism, and areas of focus. During a personalized session, she may draw from one tradition or intuitively combine several. You do not need to select a particular tradition before scheduling.

  • Usui Reiki — foundational, gentle, grounding; often supports relaxation, balance, clarity, and spiritual awareness.
  • Tera Mai Seichem Reiki — qualities associated with earth, water, fire, air, and spirit; may support grounding, emotional movement, clearing, and vitality.
  • Shamballa Reiki — strong, transformative quality; may support work with deeply rooted emotions and guarded or stagnant patterns.
  • Lotus Light Reiki — soft, nurturing quality associated with Divine Mother energy; may support self-love, tenderness, and inner-child healing.
  • Kundalini Reiki — supports energetic activation and flow within a carefully held session container.

Reiki training, attunements, and certification are also available. Students generally need Usui Reiki Level Two before certification in additional systems—please inquire about prerequisites.
