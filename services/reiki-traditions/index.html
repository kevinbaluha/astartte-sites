Reiki Traditions

Asttarte is trained in several Reiki traditions, each with its own energetic qualities, symbolism and areas of focus. During a personalized session, she may draw from one tradition or intuitively combine several according to your intentions and what feels most supportive.

Reiki may be offered through light touch, with hands positioned above the body or through a combination chosen with your consent. You do not need to select a particular Reiki tradition before scheduling.

Reiki Healing

Traditional Reiki

Reiki is a gentle Japanese energy-healing practice offered through light touch or with the practitioner’s hands positioned above the body. Sessions may support relaxation, stress reduction, self-awareness and spiritual connection. Asttarte draws from her extensive Reiki training while adapting each session to the individual.

Usui Reiki

Healing from the inside out.

Usui Reiki is the foundational Reiki tradition and the basis for many systems that developed afterward. Its gentle, grounding quality may support relaxation, energetic balance, clarity and spiritual awareness.

  • Lotus Light Reiki — soft, nurturing Divine Mother quality; self-love, tenderness, inner-child healing.
  • Tera Mei Seichem — earth, water, fire, air, and spirit qualities.
  • Shamballa Reiki — strong, transformative quality for deep patterns.
  • Kundalini Reiki — carefully held energetic activation and flow.

Training, attunements, and certification inquiries are welcome.
