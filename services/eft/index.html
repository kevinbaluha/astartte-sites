EFT

Intuitive EFT tapping and holding for grief, trauma memory, breakups, emotional blocks, relationship patterns, and stress held in the body.

Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT / tapping) combines focused awareness with tapping or holding on acupressure points to support emotional regulation, stress relief, and shifting difficult emotional patterns.

Over nearly two decades of client work, Asttarte has developed an intuitive style of EFT that may include tapping, holding, breath, and verbal processing. People seek this work for grief, trauma-related memories, breakups, challenging emotions, blocks to freedom, relationship patterns, and stress that shows up physically.

Sessions are paced carefully and grounded in consent. EFT may be offered on its own or woven into coaching and energy-healing sessions.
